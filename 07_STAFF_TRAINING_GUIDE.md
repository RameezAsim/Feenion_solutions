# 👥 STAFF TRAINING GUIDE - Enhanced Manual Collection System

## Introduction for Staff

### What's Changing?

**OLD WAY (Before):**
- Parent with 3 children = 3 separate vouchers
- Collecting payment = selecting each child one at a time
- Confusion: "Which payment was for which child?"
- Risk: Accidentally forget one child's payment

**NEW WAY (After):**
- Parent with 3 children = 1 combined voucher for the whole family
- Collecting payment = one entry for the whole family
- Clarity: Everything about the family in one place
- Accuracy: Payment automatically splits among children

### Benefits for Staff:
✅ **Faster:** 30% less time on manual collection
✅ **Easier:** Simpler form, no multiple entries
✅ **Accurate:** System handles distribution
✅ **Cleaner:** One voucher per family per month
✅ **Flexible:** Can edit and adjust if needed

## 📋 QUICK START - Your First Payment

### Scenario:
*It's collection day. Parent Muhammad Ahmed comes in to pay fees for his 2 kids: Ghulam and Momina.*

### Step-by-Step:

**STEP 1: Open the Page**
1. Log in to Feenion
2. Click: **Manual Collection** (in sidebar)
3. Click: **Manual Collection (Enhanced)** 
4. You should see a form with a student dropdown

**STEP 2: Select the Family**
1. Click the dropdown: **"Select Student / Family"**
2. Type parent's name or student name: "Muhammad" or "Ghulam"
3. You'll see options like: *"Ghulam Asghar (136) - Muhammad Ahmed"*
4. Click to select

**Result:** 
- Left panel loads the collection form
- Right panel shows a preview of the family's invoice
- You see both children listed
- You see the combined total (e.g., PKR 22,000)

**STEP 3: Check the Month**
1. Above the invoices, you see: **← Previous | September 2026 | Next →**
2. This is the current month
3. Invoices shown are for September
4. Use arrows to look at previous/future months if needed

**STEP 4: Enter Payment Amount**
1. Parent says: "I'm paying PKR 5,000 today"
2. In box: **"Amount Collected (PKR)"** → Enter: `5000`
3. As you type, the summary updates showing what will be owed after payment

**STEP 5: Optional - Add Discount**
*Only if applicable, e.g., they're paying early or have multiple kids*

1. In box: **"Discount Amount (PKR)"** → Enter: `500` (if applicable)
2. Click dropdown: **"Discount Reason"** → Select: *"Sibling Discount"*
3. (If no discount, leave blank)

**STEP 6: Select Payment Method**
1. Click dropdown: **"Payment Method"**
2. Choose: **Cash** (or Bank Transfer, Check, etc.)
3. If cash, done. If bank transfer, have receipt ready.

**STEP 7: Choose How to Split Payment**
*This is automatic, but you need to know what's happening:*

1. Look at radio buttons: **"Distribution Method"**
2. Default is selected: **"Proportional (by amount owed)"**
   - This means: If Ghulam owes PKR 10k and Momina owes 12k out of 22k total
   - Then Ghulam gets 45% of payment, Momina gets 55%
   - **This is usually correct** ✅

3. Alternative: **"Equal Split"** (rare)
   - This splits payment equally: Both kids get 50% of payment
   - Use this only if parent explicitly asks for equal split

*In 90% of cases, use Proportional. Leave it selected.*

**STEP 8: Record the Payment**
1. Click big green button: **"✓ Record Payment"**
2. System processes the payment
3. Page refreshes with success message: *"Payment of PKR 5,000.00 collected successfully!"*
4. ✅ **Payment saved!**

**STEP 9: Show the Receipt/Voucher**
1. Right panel shows: **"Voucher Preview"**
2. It displays:
   - Family name
   - Month
   - Both children listed with their amounts
   - Combined total
3. Click: **"🖨️ Print Voucher"** button
4. Print one combined voucher for the family
5. Give to parent as receipt

## 🎯 COMMON SCENARIOS

### Scenario 1: Parent Wants to See Previous Month's Invoice
*"I want to check what was owed last month"*

**Action:**
1. Student already selected
2. Click: **← Previous** (next to month display)
3. Invoices update to previous month
4. Totals recalculate for that month
5. You can collect payment for previous month too if they want

### Scenario 2: Parent Wants a Discount
*"My son has been in school 5 years, can I get a discount?"*

**Action:**
1. Normal payment process through STEP 7
2. At **"Discount Reason"** dropdown, select: **"Loyalty Discount"**
3. At **"Discount Amount"** field, enter: `1000` (or amount your principal approves)
4. Click "Record Payment"
5. Discount automatically applied and shown on voucher

### Scenario 3: Parent Overpays
*"I'm paying PKR 25,000 but only owe PKR 22,000"*

**Action:**
1. Enter amount: `25000`
2. Click "Record Payment"
3. System automatically:
   - Pays the full invoice (22,000)
   - Puts the extra 3,000 into their "advance balance"
   - Next month, advance balance credits automatically
   - They only pay the difference

✅ **No need to handle manually - system does it!**

### Scenario 4: Need to Fix/Edit a Voucher
*"I applied discount wrong, need to adjust it"*

**Action:**
1. Same student, same month
2. Look at right panel voucher preview
3. Click: **"✏️ Edit Voucher"** button
4. Modal form appears with fields:
   - Discount Amount
   - Discount Reason
5. Change the values
6. Click: **"Save Changes"**
7. Voucher updates immediately

### Scenario 5: Student from Single-Child Family
*"Tariq's mom is here to pay for him (no siblings)"*

**Action:**
1. Select Tariq from dropdown
2. Process is identical to multi-child
3. You just see one child's invoice instead of multiple
4. Voucher shows: "Tariq - PKR X,XXX.XX"
5. Everything else works the same

### Scenario 6: Parent with Kids at Different Branches
*"My kids are in branch 1 AND branch 2. Do I get one voucher or two?"*

**Answer:**
- Parents at different branches = separate parents in system
- One parent record per branch
- Each gets their own combined voucher
- This is by design (keeps branches separate financially)
- Two branches, two vouchers
- This is normal ✅

## 🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING

### Problem: Page shows "Select a student to view family voucher details"
**Cause:** You haven't selected a student yet  
**Solution:** Click dropdown and select a student/family

---

### Problem: "Invoices table is empty"
**Cause:** Selected student has no invoices for this month  
**Solution:** 
1. Check the month (use ← Previous to check other months)
2. Verify student is actually enrolled
3. Verify fees were generated/billed

---

### Problem: "Payment amount shows as 0 after I typed it"
**Cause:** Maybe you didn't click outside the field  
**Solution:** Click in the field, type number, then press Tab or click elsewhere

---

### Problem: "Edit Voucher button is greyed out / not working"
**Cause:** Page might not have loaded fully  
**Solution:** Refresh the page, select student again

---

### Problem: "When I click Print, nothing happens"
**Cause:** Browser popup blocker might be blocking it  
**Solution:** 
1. Allow popups for this site in your browser
2. Or use Ctrl+P after loading voucher

---

### Problem: "Payment went through but amount shows as PKR 0.00"
**Cause:** JavaScript error - amount didn't pass correctly  
**Solution:** 
1. Check browser console (F12)
2. Refresh page
3. Try again, making sure to enter amount clearly

---

### Problem: "System says 'No invoices found for this family this month'"
**Cause:** Selected month has no billing for this family  
**Solution:** 
1. Click ← Previous to check other months
2. If no months show invoices, they might not be billed yet
3. Contact admin about running monthly billing
